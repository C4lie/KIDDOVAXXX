Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • KiddoVax Clinical Health Platform

1. Information We Collect

KiddoVax collects essential clinical and personal information necessary to manage pediatric immunization schedules, clinic appointment reservations, and automated reminder alerts:

  • Parent / Guardian Information: Full name, verified mobile contact number, residential locality, and authentication credentials.
  • Child Immunization Profile: Child name, date of birth, blood group, gender, and historic vaccination records.
  • Clinical Transaction Data: Appointment bookings, hospital verifications, RFID attendance logs, and authorized medical practitioner notes.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your medical and personal information is strictly utilized for the following healthcare purposes:

  • Calculating age-appropriate Universal Immunization Programme (UIP) milestone schedules.
  • Dispatching critical SMS and dashboard alerts for upcoming or delayed vaccination doses.
  • Enabling authorized hospital staff and pediatricians to record administered vaccine batches securely.
  • Generating digital vaccination passports and printable immunization records for school admissions.

3. Data Security & Confidentiality

We implement industry-standard cryptographic protocols to protect electronic health records (EHR). Passwords are encrypted using cryptographic hashing algorithms, and all communication between your browser and our servers is secured with TLS/SSL encryption. We do not sell, rent, or trade patient health information to third-party advertisers.

4. Parental Rights & Data Access

Parents and legal guardians have the right to access, review, correct, or request the export of their child's digital vaccination history at any time through the KiddoVax portal.
